Frequently Asked Questions

Setup
HertzCast works with any Yamaha AV receiver that supports the Yamaha Extended Control (YXC) API — typically models from 2015 onwards with network connectivity. If your receiver works with the official Yamaha MusicCast Controller app, it will work with HertzCast.
Open HertzCast, go to Settings, and enter your receiver's local IP address. You can find the IP in your router's device list or in the receiver's own network settings menu.
Make sure your Mac and your receiver are on the same Wi-Fi network.
No. HertzCast communicates exclusively with your receiver over your local network. No internet connection is required, and no data ever leaves your home network.
Connection

A few things to verify:

  • Your Mac and receiver are on the same Wi-Fi network (not guest vs. main)
  • The IP address in Settings matches your receiver's current IP
  • Your receiver is powered on and fully booted
  • No firewall or router rule is blocking local HTTP traffic on port 80
IP addresses on home networks can change when a device reconnects. Consider assigning a static IP to your receiver in your router settings.
HertzCast displays exactly what your receiver reports. If track info or album art is missing, it means your receiver isn't providing it for the current input or source. This is normal for some inputs (e.g. FM Tuner, Bluetooth) that don't transmit metadata.
